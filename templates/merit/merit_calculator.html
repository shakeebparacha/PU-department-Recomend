Merit Calculation Formulas
1st Semester BS (4-Yrs) Program :
Merit = ((((Matric_Obtained × 0.25) + Inter_Obtained + Additional_Marks) / ((Matric_Total × 0.25) + Inter_Total)) × 75) + ((Entry_Test_Obtained / Entry_Test_Total) × 25)
BS 5th Semester or Equivalent:
Merit % = (Marks_Earned / Total_Marks) × 100

Where:
Marks_Earned = (Matric_Obtained × 0.25) + (Inter_Obtained × 0.20) + BA_BSc_Obtained + Additional_Marks
Total_Marks = (Matric_Total × 0.25) + (Inter_Total × 0.20) + BA_BSc_Total
Rules:
  • Additional_Marks: This is 20 for Hafiz-e-Quran candidates, otherwise 0.
  • 1st Semester (2nd Year): Academic Component (75%) + Entry Test Component (25%)
  • 5th Semester: Weighted average of Matric (25%), Inter (20%), and BA/BSc (55%) components
  • Final Result: Rounded to two decimal places
